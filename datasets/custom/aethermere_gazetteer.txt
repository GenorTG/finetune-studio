# GAZETTEER AND REGISTER OF AETHERMERE
## Being a Compendium of Places, Persons, Offices, Prices, and Peculiarities

---

## I. THE CITIES

### Vantage
The capital and largest city, population 41,000, founded Year 1 on the eastern rim harbor. Wards, from the sea inward: the Harbor Ward (fish-market, the Admiralty Court, the Customs House built 512), the Lower Ward (the Silo of Gratitude museum, the weavers' Row of Twelve licensed houses), the Middle Ward (the Council Spire, 200 feet tall; the Cathedral of the Ring and its two-ton Remembrance bell), and the Upper Ward (the Vault of Quiet, the Queen's Winter Palace, and the Ledger Halls where the 412 original volumes of the Ledger of Memory are kept under triple lock). Vantage's stone seawall is 40 feet high, 12 feet thick at the base, and unbroken since Year 89. The city's night-watch numbers 300, paid 25 crowns a year; its municipal budget is 400,000 crowns.

### Brindle
Second city, population 22,000, founded Year 3 at the site of the great Gorath breach. Seat of the present Court: the Hall of Seals, where Regent-Queen Maren holds court each Bloommonth. Home of the Sealers' Monument (nine feet, fused chitin, forty names) and the annual Remembrance Day on 17th Frostmonth. Brindle's famous industry is the chitin-yard: recovered Gorath shell is worked into armor plate (a breastplate costs 120 crowns), blade springs, and the famous chitin-lamps that burn without oil.

### Greyhallow
Population 8,400. The salt-town. Its pans produce 4,000 pounds of threshold-salt yearly at 2 crowns the pound — the backbone of the threshold trade mandated by the Umbral Annex. Greyhallow is also the source of the brine-cured eel that is the only food permitted inside the Cormorant Quarantine barge. The town's Salt-Guild master is called the Panfather; the present Panfather is Doran Whit, elected for life in 1654.

### Cormorant (Quarantined)
Population 8,647 descendants, sealed since Year 641. Ruled by the Door-Wardens under Warden-Marshal Cryss Halloway (the 31st to hold the office). Supply barge monthly. No exit for the born-inside; the fine is 10,000 crowns and Binding. The city's doors all still bear their salt grooves, renewed monthly by the Door-Wardens at a cost of 200 pounds of salt a month.

### Pennhallow
Rim-town of 3,200 at the disc's western edge, last stop before the Void Sea. Site of Ilse Vantorre's last verified sighting (1147) and of the Unburdened's first chapel, now a barn. The Pennhallow light — a driftglass beacon — is the westernmost lit object on the disc.

- Chitin breastplate: 120 crowns

- Binding (the operation): 500 crowns, paid to the College of Gravitation
- A Husk-cull bounty: 15 crowns per confirmed Husk, paid by any Warden
- Crown budget: 2,100,000 crowns/yr; Vantage's share 400,000

## IV. THE HAZARDS REGISTER (as kept by the Marches Wardens)

1. **Husk packs** — the Sallow Marches; 3 packs sighted this year, largest 14 strong.
2. **Driftmass phase-outs** — 2 ships lost in the last five years, 61 souls.
3. **Gorath seismicity** — the crust groans in the Brindle reach; wardens inspect the sealed shafts twice a year, at Seedmonth and Frostmonth.
4. **Umbral seepage** — any door whose salt groove goes wet must be reported to the nearest Remembrancer within three days; failure is a 50-crown fine.
5. **Memory-drought** — a licensed weaver who has spent more than 200 memories must present for yearly inspection; 41 weavers are currently on the Drought List.

## V. THE RING AND THE SEA

The Luminous Ring is 1,100 miles off the rim by the *Salt Psalm*'s log of 1509. No ship has gone further. The Void Sea's tide — the Pull — runs outward and takes 12 hours to turn; sailors call the turning "the Breath." The sea's depth has never been sounded; the deepest line, dropped in 1600, ran out 3,000 feet without touching bottom, off the town of Pennhallow.
