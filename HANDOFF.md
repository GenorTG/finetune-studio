5. **Three overlapping export-listing routes** (2026-10-01 audit): `training.py`'s `GET /runs/{run_id}/exports` (disk-scan, not project-scoped) duplicates `exports.py`'s DB-backed, project-scoped equivalent. Needs a decision on which to keep.
