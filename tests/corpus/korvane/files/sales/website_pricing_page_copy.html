What is a container-day, exactly?

One tagged container being monitored for one calendar day counts as one container-day, and a day is counted from 00:00 to 24:00 Central European Time. A container that is tagged at 23:50 counts for that day as well, which we find charming and our finance team finds necessary. Unused container-days do not roll over to the next month. If you regularly use more than 600 container-days on Fairway, ask us about Deep Water, because beyond about 1,100 container-days a month the arithmetic starts to favour a custom plan.

How fast can we start?

Most customers see their first live alerts typically within 5 weeks of signing. Customers with their own hubs on site, such as Fruttoria Baltica, who started with 120 tags and now run 410, tend to be quicker. Month-to-month contracts are possible on Harbour only, at a surcharge of 18%. If you pay an invoice within 8 days we take 1.2% off it, no questions asked.
