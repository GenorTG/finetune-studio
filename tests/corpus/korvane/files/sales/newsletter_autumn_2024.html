The Korvane Courier: Autumn 2024

Issue 9 · Sent 1 October 2024 · Written by Lotte Sandberg, Marketing · Reading time: about 6 minutes (we timed Rafał, he took 11)

Hello from the Mothership

Autumn is when the citrus ships come in, the berry people go quiet and the whole depot smells faintly of diesel and oranges. It is also, apparently, when marketing gets to send a newsletter that is longer than anybody asked for. Sorry. There is a lot to tell you, so here is the short list before the long one: a new customer story, a better trade-in offer for old KC-Tag 3 owners, a price change you should know about before 1 November, and three dates for your diary.

Customer story: Fruttoria Baltica and the end of Monday panic

On 12 September we published the story of Fruttoria Baltica, the citrus importer from Pruszcz Gdański, who now run 410 KC-Tag 4 tags across their network. Their Director of Logistics, Ewelina Marchwicka, told us that excursion alerts at her company fell from 6.8 to 1.9 per 1,000 container-days between 2022 and the first half of 2024. We have put the PDF on the customers page, and the first 200 downloads came in the first 36 hours, which our web person described as "slightly alarming".

Product news

Tidewalk 6.4 arrives on 21 October

The next minor release of Tidewalk goes live for all customers on Monday 21 October 2024, outside the dispatch peak, between 05:00 and 06:30 CET. The headline feature is bulk geofence import: you can now upload a whole file of yard and terminal polygons at once instead of drawing them one by one. Dispatchers also get saved views, so the night shift can keep its own list of containers separate from the day shift. Nothing about your alert rules changes. Release notes will be in the app from the morning of the release.

Tidewalk 7 beta: save the date

The first customers will be invited to the Tidewalk 7 beta in spring 2025. Applications open on 6 January 2025 and we have 40 places. If you are a Fairway or Deep Water customer, your account manager will contact you anyway; Harbour customers can apply through the form in the app.

Autumn tag swap: more for your old loggers

Still have KC-Tag 3 loggers in a drawer or, worse, in service? This autumn we credit EUR 61 for each KC-Tag 3 you send back when you buy KC-Tag 4. The offer runs until 30 November 2024, needs a minimum of 20 tags per return shipment and includes a prepaid return label from anywhere in Poland, Germany and the Nordics. It replaces the summer credit of EUR 45 per tag, which expired on 30 September, so please do not quote the old figure to your purchasing department. The credit is deducted from the hardware invoice, not paid out in cash.

List prices change on 1 November

As announced in the summer, our list prices rise by 3.4% on 1 November 2024. From that date Tidewalk Harbour costs EUR 360.87 per month and Tidewalk Fairway EUR 1,230.46 per month; a KC-Tag 4 at the lowest volume tier is EUR 122.01. Quotes issued before 1 November keep their prices if signed within their 30-day validity. If you have been meaning to renew early, now is the moment, and your account manager will not pretend to be surprised.
