Re: Observation from our GDP self-inspection of 17 September 2024 - missing calibration certificates

Dear Mr Nowogrodzki,

In the course of our annual GDP self-inspection on 17 September 2024 we verified the calibration records of the temperature loggers used on pharmaceutical shipments handled under our contract with Korvane. For 14 of the KC-Tag 4 units, serial numbers 20417 to 20430 from batch KT4-R2, no calibration certificate could be found in our quality system. Your delivery note of 11 June 2024 lists the certificates as "enclosed", but they were not in the shipment received by our goods-in department.

Under our quality agreement every logger used on a GDP shipment must carry a valid calibration certificate and be recalibrated at least every 12 months. Until the certificates are produced, we have placed the 14 units in quarantine and have removed them from active use. We do not consider this a critical deviation, but it is a major observation under our own SOP and it will be reported as such to our Responsible Person.

We therefore request: (1) copies of the calibration certificates for all 14 units; (2) a corrective and preventive action (CAPA) response from Korvane, to reach us no later than 18 October 2024; and (3) confirmation that the 12-month recalibration cycle has been applied to every KC-Tag 4 delivered to us since the start of 2024.

We are mindful that our team's audit of Korvane is scheduled for 5 November 2024, and we would prefer to treat this matter as closed by then. Please note that the extension of our cooperation, currently under discussion, depends on a satisfactory response.

Yours sincerely,

Dr. Annelie Roth
Head of Quality Assurance
Helvetia Pharma Logistik GmbH
