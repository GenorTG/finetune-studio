KC-Tag 4 – wireless logger for reefer containers

Know what your cargo is living through. The KC-Tag 4 sits quietly on the door frame of a reefer, measures temperature and humidity around the clock and tells you the moment a door is opened, whether or not anybody is looking. Built in Gdynia, tested in a freezer that is colder than your customers' complaints.

The KC-Tag 4 is the successor of the KC-Tag 3 and works with the KC-Hub 200 gateway in our depots and in the yards of partner terminals. Readings are forwarded to Tidewalk, where alarms, reports and audit trails are generated for you automatically. The current hardware revision is R2, available from 9 October 2023. Units shipped before that date are revision R1 and are not covered by this sheet.
