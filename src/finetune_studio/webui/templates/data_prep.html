    Preference pairs (DPO)
    
      
    An optional second training stage that teaches the model what a good answer looks like next to a bad one.
    
    
    
    0 approved pairs
  
    
  
    
    Each preference pair is one question with two answers: the chosen one (your approved, source-faithful answer
    or an honest “not covered”) and a rejected one (what a model writes when it guesses). Training on them
    pushes the model away from inventing facts.
  
    
  
    
    When to use it: after the normal fine-tune (SFT) has taught the facts, to curb hallucination and teach the
    model to say “that isn’t in the documents”. It needs approved pairs above and the helper model loaded
    (it writes the rejected answers). Not a replacement for SFT.
